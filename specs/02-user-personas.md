# User Personas

## 1. Primary Customer: Value-Seeking Discovery Buyer
### Persona
A customer who discovers the brand through organic search, Amazon, or social channels and values product utility, trust, and clear information.

### Goals
- understand the product quickly
- compare variants or options
- feel confident before purchasing
- receive clear shipping and return information
- complete a purchase with minimal friction

### Pain Points
- unclear product details
- lack of trust in a new brand
- poor mobile shopping experience
- complicated checkout flow
- weak post-purchase support

### Needs
- trustworthy brand presentation
- clear product education
- simple cart and checkout
- fast mobile experience

## 2. Repeat Customer: Brand Loyal Buyer
### Persona
A customer who has already purchased once and returns for replenishment or additional products.

### Goals
- quickly reorder
- view order history and account details
- manage profile and preferences
- receive timely support or updates

### Needs
- account management
- order visibility
- dependable fulfillment communication

## 3. Founder / Business Operator
### Persona
One of the three founders responsible for catalog, merchandising, performance, and launch decisions.

### Goals
- update products and pricing
- review sales and operational metrics
- react quickly to stock and order issues
- monitor customer trust indicators

### Needs
- admin dashboard
- operational visibility
- accessible business workflows
- minimal operational complexity

## 4. Operations / Fulfillment Owner
### Persona
A team member responsible for order flow, inventory management, and shipping coordination.

### Goals
- maintain accurate inventory
- process orders and updates without errors
- identify fulfillment exceptions

### Needs
- order management tools
- inventory visibility
- clear state transitions and audit history

## 5. Support Agent
### Persona
Staff member handling customer requests and return issues.

### Goals
- resolve order or product issues quickly
- access customer data without unnecessary exposure
- document and audit interactions appropriately

### Needs
- customer order view
- account and support details
- clear escalation path

## 6. Admin / Platform Administrator
### Persona
A platform administrator with elevated access to manage configuration and security-sensitive settings.

### Goals
- maintain site configuration and access rules
- review audit logs
- support security reviews

### Needs
- RBAC-protected admin views
- audit log visibility
- controlled privileged actions
